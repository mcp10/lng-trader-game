LNG Trader Sim

Buy FOB at Sabine Pass, Ras Laffan, Gladstone or Bonny. Pick a ship, a destination and a canal. Carry the boil-off, the hire and the tolls, and land it against the curve. Manage a chartered fleet through two winters and earn your stars.

TTF · JKM · Henry Hub · Brent · 174k spot charter
Starts October 2026 with $250M of trading capital and one TFDE on a one-year term charter at Sabine Pass. Progress saves in this browser.

Cargoes

A weekly offer book at four load ports, each tender with its own laycan and price. Every lifting shows freight, boil-off, canal and port costs before you confirm; FOB is paid at loading. Divert on the water, fix the price forward, float it in contango, or sell it afloat.

Chartered fleet

Steam, TFDE and 2-stroke tonnage on term or spot. Term ships cost hire every day, idle or not, and need positioning. Spot ships are prompt but priced round-trip. Relet idle tonnage when the spot market spikes.

Contracts and windows

Sell DES into a two-week delivery window and source the cargo later, or sign term contracts: a US tolling slot you can cancel for the fee, a take-or-pay Qatar SPA, a Japanese oil-linked sale. Collateral, credit breaches and default penalties are all real.

Market and paper

Seasonal forward curves with hub liquidity that thins when the tape is volatile, TTF and JKM paper to W+26 with margin, EU storage, canal status, and a news feed that lands one week before prices move.

Educational and entertainment simulation. Prices, ports and events are stylised and not a forecast. Not financial advice.
